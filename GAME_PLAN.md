# The Legend of ToneDa: GlockNina of Time

An original, Nintendo 64-inspired 3D fantasy action-adventure for Big Bang Arcade.

## Development status
Pre-production / prototype setup. No playable build has been released yet.

## Initial milestone: Trap Village
- Browser-based 3D game (desktop first, mobile controls later)
- Low-poly retro fantasy village, elevated wooden TRAP house, Great Trap Tree
- YN ToneDa character, movement, camera, jumping, combat, shield
- Niggy, a red bat-winged demon companion with voice-triggered typewriter dialogue
- Seed-shooting tree and acorn enemies called Opps
- Musical GlockNina mechanic, with fantasy sound cues and Song of Time transformation
- Dirt bike and expanded world planned for later milestones

## Audio and artwork
Original user-supplied character art and recordings, including Hyrule vocal/instrumental, ToneDa voice pack and Niggy voice pack. Assets are **not** committed yet. Confirm ownership and keep original masters in a private asset store before integration.

## Technical direction
Three.js + Vite + TypeScript, with gamepad/keyboard support and retro low-resolution rendering.

## Development sequence
1. Title screen and audio system
2. Trap Village level and camera/movement
3. Companion dialogue system
4. Opps and combat
5. First quest and music mechanic
6. Trailer capture and Big Bang Arcade integration

## Intellectual property
Create original locations, meshes, textures, sounds, and UI; use classic 3D adventure conventions for inspiration without copying Nintendo game assets.
