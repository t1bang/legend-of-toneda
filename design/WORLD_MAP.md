# Legend of ToneDa — World Map Blueprint (proposed, not final canon)

## Structure
Interconnected regions, classic adventure hub-and-spoke design; original maps and architecture.

1. **Trap Village** — starting hub, elevated TRAP house, villagers, Great Trap Tree, training clearing, Niggy introduction. Connects to Opp Woods and a future central field.
2. **Opp Woods** — winding forest paths, seed-shooting tree Opps, acorn enemies, secrets, first combat tutorial and a rhythm-gated grove.
3. **Champion's Field** — future central travel hub linking village, castle road and dirt-bike routes.
4. **Crownkeep Castle** (working name) — future castle region, princess story and main-quest progression.
5. **Echo Shrine** (working name) — first music puzzle dungeon, teaches musical sequences.

## First playable quest
Spawn at TRAP house → Niggy introduction → speak to Great Trap Tree → learn movement/lock-on → defeat three Opps in the clearing → receive clue about the Song of Time.

## Level design rules
Readable landmarks, looping paths, hidden rewards, traversal shortcuts, controller-friendly camera. Each region should have its own silhouettes, palette, audio and NPCs.

**Approval needed:** Region names beyond Trap Village, Trap House, Great Trap Tree and Opps are proposals.
